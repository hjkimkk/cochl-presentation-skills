MISSION STATEMENT
Every device should be able to hear what is happening around it, as naturally as it sees or touches.
CONFIDENTIAL + PROPRIETARY

Machines are deaf today

Phones, cameras, cars and appliances see, move and respond, yet almost none of them understand sound.

A glass break, a baby crying, a siren or a leaking pipe goes unnoticed because the machine has no sense of hearing.

Devices can see. They cannot hear.Hearing is the missing sense in machine perception.
