Machines cannot hear today

Cameras, voice assistants and sensors have given machines sight and speech. The sounds around them, from a breaking window to a siren, still pass by unnoticed.

Without hearing, devices miss events that people notice instantly, which limits what smart home, automotive and safety products can do on their own.

Devices are deaf to the world around them.
Cochl.Sense changes that.
